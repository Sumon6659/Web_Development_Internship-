Welcome to PUST

Nurturing Future Leaders in Science, Business & Humanities

Pundra University of Science and Technology (PUST), established in 2001 and officially approved by the Government of Bangladesh and the University Grants Commission (UGC) in 2002, is the pioneer private higher education institution in North Bengal.

Dedicated to closing regional educational gaps, PUST delivers internationally benchmarked undergraduate and postgraduate education, combining theoretical rigor with practical laboratory experience and ethical values.

✓ UGC and Ministry of Education approved curriculum
✓ Experienced faculty from leading national and global institutions
✓ Up to 100% merit waivers and North Bengal regional scholarships

Why Choose PUST

The Preferred Higher Education Destination in North Bengal

Verified institutional advantages making Pundra University a premier choice for students across Bangladesh.

01

UGC & Government Approval

Established in 2001 and approved under the Private University Act, all academic programs comply strictly with University Grants Commission criteria.

02

Outcome-Based Education (OBE)

Modern curriculum designed around practical learning outcomes, industry competencies, problem-solving skills, and global standards.

03

Qualified Teaching Faculty

Distinguished permanent faculty supported by adjunct scholars from renowned public engineering and research institutions.

04

Modern Engineering & Computing Labs

Well-equipped computer laboratories, electronics testbenches, surveying instruments, and civil engineering testing equipment.

05

Generous Scholarships & Waivers

Merit waivers up to 100% for GPA 5.00 achievers, North Bengal regional quotas, female student stipends, and freedom-fighter family provisions.

06

Green & Accessible Campus

Conveniently located on Rangpur Road in Bogura with spacious academic buildings, central library, sports grounds, and student amenities.
