Academic Framework

Outcome-Based Education (OBE) System

At Pundra University of Science and Technology, academic programs are structured in full compliance with the University Grants Commission (UGC) Outcome-Based Education (OBE) template. This modern paradigm shifts focus from conventional memorization to demonstrative mastery of learning outcomes, analytical problem solving, and real-world competencies.

Courses are organized on a transparent credit-hour semester system, incorporating continuous in-semester evaluation, midterm assessments, term papers, laboratory practicals, and comprehensive final examinations.

✓ International standard OBE grading and course credit system
✓ Extensive laboratory sessions alongside theoretical lectures
✓ Tutorial sessions, mentorship, and career placement support
