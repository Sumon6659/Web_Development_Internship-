About Pundra University

The First Private University in North Bengal, Dedicated to Academic Excellence and Technological Advancement Since 2001.

Institution Overview

A Historic Legacy of Higher Education in North Bengal

Pundra University of Science and Technology (PUST), also known institutionally as PUB, was established in 2001 in Bogura, Bangladesh. It stands proudly as the pioneer private higher education institution in the northern region of the country, established to make world-class science, technical, business, and humanist education accessible to all.

Formally approved by the Ministry of Education, Government of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, and the University Grants Commission (UGC) in 2002, PUST functions under the Private University Act with strict adherence to national academic benchmarks and international Outcome-Based Education (OBE) frameworks.

Located on its own permanent campus at Rangpur Road, Baghopara, Gokul, Bogura, the university provides an expansive green campus environment where students combine rigorous curriculum study with hands-on laboratory practice.

Verified Institutional Milestone

Chronicle of Pundra University

Key historical milestones documenting the growth of PUST since its founding as North Bengal's first private university.

2001

Foundation of Pundra University

Pundra University of Science and Technology was founded in Bogura by dedicated educators and philanthropists committed to democratizing modern higher education across Northern Bangladesh.

2002

Government & UGC Formal Approval

The Government of Bangladesh and the University Grants Commission (UGC) officially accorded statutory approval to PUST to conduct degree programs under the Private University Act.

2003 - 2010

Faculty Expansion & Academic Breadth

Inception of foundational academic faculties including Science & Engineering, Business Studies, and Humanities, introducing Computer Science, EEE, BBA, and English programs.

2011 - 2020

Permanent Campus & Laboratory Infrastructure

Relocation to its permanent university campus on Rangpur Road, Baghopara, Bogura, equipped with modern engineering laboratories, central library, and digital computing facilities.

2021 - Present

Adoption of OBE Framework & Modern Research

Comprehensive adoption of the UGC Outcome-Based Education (OBE) curriculum model across all departments, enhancing student competencies and national accreditation standards.

Core Principles

Educational Goals & Core Values

The foundational principles that guide teaching, faculty research, student life, and administration at PUST.

01

Academic Integrity

Upholding uncompromising standards of intellectual honesty, rigorous examination evaluation, and ethical scholarship across all degrees.

02

Scientific Inquiry

Instilling curiosity, experimentation, data-driven analytical thinking, and technological innovation in engineering and applied sciences.

03

Regional Empowerment

Bridging educational disparities by offering affordable, world-class degree opportunities to talented youth across Northern Bangladesh.

04

Inclusivity & Equity

Ensuring supportive, merit-oriented admissions with extensive financial aid waivers for meritorious and underprivileged learners.

Outcome-Based Learning

Aligning classroom objectives directly with practical workplace capabilities, professional certifications, and lifelong employability.

Social Responsibility

Inspiring students to serve their local communities, uphold human rights, protect the environment, and contribute to Bangladesh's development.
