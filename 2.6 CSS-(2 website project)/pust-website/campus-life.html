Campus Life & Facilities

A Dynamic, Enriching Student Community at Our Permanent Campus in Bogura, Bangladesh.

Student Experience

Beyond the Classroom: A Holistic Environment

Life at Pundra University of Science and Technology is about much more than attending lectures. Located in the quiet, picturesque landscape of Baghopara, Bogura, our campus provides an energetic atmosphere where intellectual curiosity meets extracurricular engagement.

Students build lifelong friendships, explore creative passions through active university clubs, organize technical hackathons, participate in moot court trials, and represent PUST in inter-university athletic competitions.

✓ Active student clubs spanning computing, culture, sports, and debates
✓ Regular academic workshops, leadership summits, and cultural celebrations
✓ Peaceful green campus with modern library, cafeteria, and sports fields

Co-Curricular Activities

Student Clubs & Societies

Join student-led organizations that foster teamwork, professional skills, cultural identity, and social service.

PUST Computer Club (PUSTCC)

Organizes inter-university programming contests, hackathons, open-source workshops, web development bootcamps, and tech talks led by industry mentors.

Cultural & Literary Forum

Celebrates national heritage events including Pohela Boishakh, International Mother Language Day, Victory Day, seasonal drama productions, and music fests.

Debating & Moot Court Society

Hones public speaking, parliamentary debating, legal argumentation, and critical discourse through weekly debate sessions and simulated appellate trials.

PUST Sports Club

Hosts annual intra-university cricket and football championships, badminton tournaments, table tennis leagues, and fitness initiatives across all departments.

Young Entrepreneurs Club

Connects business and engineering students with startup incubators, venture mentors, case-study competitions, and corporate interview prep workshops.

Social Welfare & Blood Donor Unit

Coordinates emergency voluntary blood donation drives, flood relief campaigns, tree plantation projects, and community winter clothing distributions in Bogura.

Campus Traditions

Annual Campus Milestones & Festivals

Signature traditions that bring together students, faculty, alumni, and honorable guests throughout the academic year.

01

Freshers’ Reception

A grand welcoming festival for incoming batches, featuring cultural music, talent performances, and inspiring addresses by the Vice-Chancellor.

02

Annual Sports Week

A spirited multi-day tournament pitting departments against each other in cricket, football, sprint relays, chess, and tug-of-war.

03

National Heritage Days

Vibrant observance of Bangladesh's historic milestones, including Independence Day, Victory Day, and Ekushey February Language Day.

04

Pohela Boishakh Fest

Traditional Bengali New Year celebration featuring colorful folk processions, Panta-Ilish feasts, stalls, and rural artistic displays.

05

Tech & Science Fair

Engineering students display innovative hardware prototypes, robotics projects, software applications, and research posters.

06

University Convocation

The solemn conferral of academic degrees celebrating graduating scholars and academic medalists with national dignitaries.
