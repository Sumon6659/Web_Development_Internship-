Admissions 2026

Your Pathway to an Accredited Degree

Pundra University welcomes ambitious candidates from all education boards across Bangladesh as well as international applicants. Our admissions are governed by the guidelines of the Ministry of Education and the University Grants Commission (UGC).

Whether you are enrolling straight from HSC / A-Levels or transitioning from a Polytechnic Diploma in Engineering, PUST provides an inclusive, transparent admission process with dedicated counselors to guide your educational journey.

✓ Applications accepted for Undergraduate and Postgraduate programs
✓ Direct credit transfer options available for recognized diplomas
✓ Special fee concessions and merit waivers up to 100%

How to Apply

Simple 4-Step Admission Process

Follow these streamlined steps to successfully complete your registration at Pundra University.

1

Select Program

Review our faculties and select your degree of choice (CSE, EEE, Civil, BBA, Law, English, etc.) based on your academic background.

2

Submit Application

Complete the admission form online at the official portal or visit the campus admission desk in Baghopara, Bogura in person.

3

Document Verification

Submit original and attested photocopies of SSC & HSC marksheets, certificates, national ID/birth certificate, and photographs.

4

Registration & Dues

Complete registration fee payment, obtain your official Student ID card, and participate in the university orientation session.

Academic Prerequisites

Program Eligibility Criteria

Minimum eligibility thresholds established under the statutory rules of UGC and Ministry of Education Bangladesh.

Program / Degree Academic Background Minimum GPA / Requirement Special Criteria
B.Sc. in CSE HSC / A-Level (Science) Minimum GPA 2.50 in both SSC & HSC (or total GPA 6.00) Must have passed Mathematics & Physics
B.Sc. in EEE HSC / A-Level (Science) Minimum GPA 2.50 in both SSC & HSC (or total GPA 6.00) Must have passed Mathematics & Physics
B.Sc. in Civil Engineering HSC (Science) or 4-Year Polytechnic Diploma Minimum GPA 2.50 in SSC & HSC or CGPA 2.50 in Diploma Diploma holders receive credit waiver as per UGC rules
Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) HSC / Equivalent (Any discipline: Business, Science, Humanities) Minimum GPA 2.50 in both SSC & HSC (or total GPA 6.00) Open to all academic backgrounds
LL.B. (Honours) HSC / Equivalent (Any discipline) Minimum GPA 2.50 in both SSC & HSC (or total GPA 6.00) English language proficiency preferred
B.A. (Hons) in English / Bangla / Islamic Studies HSC / Alim / Equivalent Minimum GPA 2.50 in SSC and HSC Subject-specific minimum score in HSC
Master of Business Administration (MBA / EMBA) Graduation / Bachelor’s degree in any discipline Minimum CGPA 2.00 or 2nd Class in Bachelor's EMBA requires relevant professional job experience
Master of Public Health (MPH) MBBS / BDS / B.Sc. Nursing / Health Sciences degree Graduation from a recognized medical or health institution Healthcare practitioners prioritized
Financial Assistance

Scholarships & Tuition Waivers

PUST believes financial barriers should never obstruct academic potential. We offer generous waiver quotas.

Merit Waiver (GPA 5.00)

Up to 100%

Full tuition waiver available for students with Golden GPA 5.00 in both SSC and HSC examinations, subject to semester CGPA maintenance.

Freedom Fighter Quota

100% Tuition

As mandated by the Government of Bangladesh, qualified children of certified Freedom Fighters receive full tuition waivers across all faculties.

North Bengal Regional Waiver

Special Discount

Regional financial assistance and concessions for talented underprivileged students hailing from rural districts of Bogura and North Bengal.

Female Student Stipend

Merit Concession

Encouraging higher education and engineering participation for female students through special departmental fee concessions.

Sibling & Spouse Waiver

25% – 50%

Discount provided on tuition fees when two or more siblings or spouses study concurrently at Pundra University.

Semester Merit List

Dean's Award

Semester-wise performance-based tuition discounts awarded to top CGPA achievers in each departmental cohort.

Checklist

Required Documents for Admission

Please prepare the following documents before visiting the admission office or uploading online.

1 Original & 3 attested photocopies of SSC / Equivalent Certificate and Marksheet.
2 Original & 3 attested photocopies of HSC / Diploma / Equivalent Certificate and Marksheet.
3 4 recent passport-size and 2 stamp-size color photographs (formal lab print).
4 Photocopy of National ID Card (NID) or Birth Registration Certificate.
5 For postgraduate candidates: Original Bachelor’s degree transcripts and certificates.
6 For quota claimants: Certified Freedom Fighter certificate / tribal certificate.
