Chicken Biriyani

Time: 1 hour. Distribution:4 people

Ingrediants

  • Chal
    • basmoti chal- 500 gm
  • meat
    • chicken- 2 kg
    • doi -1 cup
  • Masala
    • alach,darchini,lobongo
    • biriyani masala -2 ca chamuch

Rannar dhap

  1. chal 30 min vijiye rakhun
  2. mangso doi and mosla diye merinate korun
  3. mangso vuna korun
  4. chal futiye 70% ranna korun
  5. layer kore dome rakhun 25 min
