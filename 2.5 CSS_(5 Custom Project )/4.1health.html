Common Diseases & Medicines

Primary medicine reference for common health problems. (Always consult a doctor before usage)

🌡️

Fever & Body Pain

জ্বর ও শরীর ব্যথা
Medicine: Paracetamol 500mg (Napa / Ace)

Dosage: 1 tablet 3 times daily after meals.

Care: Drink plenty of water and take proper rest.

🔥

Gastric & Acidity

গ্যাস্ট্রিক ও বুকজ্বালা
Medicine: Omeprazole 20mg (Seclo / Pantonix)

Dosage: 1 capsule 30 mins before breakfast.

Care: Avoid spicy, oily, and fried food.

🤧

Cold, Sneezing & Allergy

সর্দি, হাঁচি ও এলার্জি
Medicine: Fexofenadine 120mg / Cetirizine (Alatrol)

Dosage: 1 tablet once daily at night.

Care: Avoid dust, cold water, and allergens.

🤕

Headache & Migraine

মাথাব্যথা ও মাইগ্রেন
Medicine: Paracetamol + Caffeine (Napa Extra)

Dosage: 1 tablet after food when needed.

Care: Rest in a quiet dark room, drink water.

😷

Cough & Sore Throat

কাশি ও গলা ব্যথা
Medicine: Dextromethorphan Syrup / Tulsi Herbal

Dosage: 2 teaspoons 3 times daily with warm water.

Care: Gargle with warm salt water twice a day.

💧

Diarrhea & Dehydration

ডায়রিয়া ও পাতলা পায়খানা
Medicine: Oral Rehydration Salt (ORS Saline) + Zinc

Dosage: 1 pack ORS in 500ml water after each loose stool.

Care: Drink coconut water & keep hydrated.

Essential Daily Health Tips

Simple daily habits to keep yourself and your family healthy.

🚰

Drink Clean Water

Drink 2.5 to 3 liters of purified water daily to remove toxins and stay energized.

🥗

Eat Fresh Food

Include green vegetables, seasonal fruits, and reduce excessive sugar and fast food.

🏃

Daily Walking

At least 30 minutes of brisk walking or light exercise boosts your heart and immunity.

😴

Adequate Sleep

7-8 hours of regular, uninterrupted sleep is crucial for natural body recovery.
